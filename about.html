About

An engineer who stays with the job

DirtSavers, LLC is a Texas engineering firm based in Aledo, focused on retaining walls, screen walls, hardscape, shade structures, entry monuments and the foundations under them. The firm has designed more than 1,000 projects for more than 100 contractors, builders and developers, and every set of plans is engineered and sealed by Mike Marshall, P.E.

Meet Mike

Mike Marshall, P.E., is the founder of DirtSavers, serving clients throughout Texas. His work focuses on retaining walls, hardscape features, shade structures and entry monuments.

Mike began his career in construction surveying and cost estimating while attending the University of Texas at Arlington, where he graduated in 2006. He later worked for a Fortune 500 homebuilder, value engineering land development projects across the country. That background shapes how he engineers: with attention to construction methods, cost, and the people responsible for getting projects built. He has been a licensed Professional Engineer in Texas since 2011.

Mike values strong working relationships with owners, contractors and clients. He emphasizes practical designs, clear communication and planning that respects the project schedule.

Outside of work, Mike enjoys hunting, traveling with his wife and children, and cheering on his two daughters and younger son in sports and cheer.
