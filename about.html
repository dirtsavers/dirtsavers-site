DirtSavers, LLC is a Texas engineering firm based in Aledo, focused on retaining walls, screen walls, hardscape, shade structures, entry monuments and the foundations under them. Mike founded the firm in 2007. It has designed more than 1,000 projects for more than 100 contractors, builders and developers, and every set of plans is engineered and sealed by Mike Marshall, P.E.

Meet Mike

Mike Marshall, P.E., is the founder of DirtSavers, serving clients throughout Texas. His work focuses on retaining walls, hardscape features, shade structures and entry monuments.

Where he started

  • Construction surveying. Mike started out staking and laying out jobs in the field while earning his B.S. in Civil Engineering at the University of Texas at Arlington in 2006. He still draws plans with the layout crew in mind.
  • Cost estimating. Estimating taught him what drives the price of a job: materials, labor and time. His designs keep all three in view.
  • Land development. At a Fortune 500 homebuilder he value engineered land development projects across the country, looking for the design that works and costs less to build.

That background shapes how he engineers, with attention to construction methods, cost and the people responsible for getting projects built. To Mike, value engineering means finding the design that costs less to build without giving up strength, durability or safety. The savings come from better design, never from a weaker structure. He has been a licensed Professional Engineer in Texas since 2011.

Mike values strong working relationships with owners, contractors and clients. He emphasizes practical designs, clear communication and planning that respects the project schedule.

Outside of work, Mike enjoys hunting, traveling with his wife and children, and cheering on his two daughters and younger son in sports and cheer.
