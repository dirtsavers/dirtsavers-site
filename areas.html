How it works outside DFW

Location changes how field visits happen, not how the design gets done.

1. Design remotelySend plans, the geotechnical report and photos by email or upload. Questions are handled by phone, email or video call, and sealed drawings are delivered electronically.
2. Agree on observation up frontIf the project needs construction observation, the proposal spells out how: scheduled site visits, documented photo review at key stages, or a mix of both.
3. Keep the scheduleThe contractor gives notice before key stages such as pier drilling and pours, so visits or photo reviews happen before anything gets covered up.
