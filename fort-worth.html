Retaining wall engineer in Fort Worth

DirtSavers is based in Aledo, just west of Fort Worth. We engineer retaining walls, screen walls and entry monuments for contractors, builders and developers across Tarrant County, with field visits scheduled directly.

What we engineer

Retaining wallsStone, masonry, segmental block and gravity walls, including tall, tiered and pond walls, with wall schedules built from the grading on your plans.
Global stabilityChecks on tall and tiered walls, walls at ponds and walls with structures close behind, using the project geotechnical report.
Screen walls and monumentsBrick and stone screen walls, entry monuments, sign walls and columns on drilled piers.
