What we design

Every job is drawn for the builder in the field: wall schedules, pier and footing details, and notes a crew can build from.

Retaining walls

Gravity, segmental and concrete walls

  • Segmental block with geogrid
  • Masonry and stone gravity walls
  • Cantilevered concrete walls
  • Gabion and shotcrete walls
Screen walls

Brick, stone and CMU screen walls

Thin walls, piers and columns on drilled piers and grade beams, with wind design and expansion joint layout.

Entry monuments

Monuments, columns and signs

Community entries, logo columns and sign walls, including overhead features and sealed permit drawings.

Shade structures

Pavilions and shade structures

Pier foundations for manufactured shade structures, and steel or timber framing for custom pavilions and cabanas.

Field observation

Pier, footing and wall inspections

Observation of piers, grade beams, reinforcing and wall installation, with written field reports for the job file.

Letters & re-seals

Final acceptance and updated plans

Sealed final acceptance letters, updated site plans and re-seals when the layout changes during construction.

In the field

From drilled pier cages to finished stone, we follow our designs into construction.

How a job runs

Send what you have. We tell you what else is needed before we start.

STEP 1

Send the plans

Grading plan, landscape or hardscape plan, and the geotechnical report if there is one.

STEP 2

Design and calcs

Wall layout, heights, reinforcing and foundations, checked for the soil and loads on your site.

STEP 3

Sealed drawings

A P.E. sealed plan set and calculations ready for the city or owner review.

STEP 4

Observation and final letter

Field observation during construction and a sealed final acceptance letter at the end.
