How a job runs

Four steps from plans to a final letter.

STEP 1

Send the plans

Grading plan, landscape or hardscape plan, and the geotechnical report if there is one.

STEP 2

Design and calcs

We engineer the walls, monuments or structures shown on your plans.

STEP 3

Sealed drawings

You receive a P.E. sealed plan set ready for city or owner review.

STEP 4

Observation and final letter

Inspections during construction and a sealed final acceptance letter at the end.

Common questions

What do you need to get started?

The site or grading plan, the landscape or hardscape plan, and the geotechnical report if there is one. Email them to plans@dirtsavers.com or upload them on the contact page.

Do you work outside Dallas-Fort Worth?

Yes. We are based in Aledo and design projects across Texas, including Central Texas and the Austin and San Marcos area.

Do you inspect the walls during construction?

Yes. We inspect during construction and issue a sealed final acceptance letter at the end.

Can you look at a wall that is leaning or failing?

Yes. We investigate existing walls and provide repair recommendations and engineer letters.
