Case studies

A closer look at the design problems behind some of our walls and structures.

Valley View Industrial

Irving · Stone gravity retaining walls

Walls
Tall stone gravity walls, some with a building close behind them.
Challenge
The walls were tall enough that the question was not only whether the wall would hold, but whether the whole hillside behind and under it would hold.
Solution
Global stability checked at several sections along the tallest runs, with separate wall schedules for building surcharge and for the higher walls.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Acorn North Ph 2

Princeton · Tiered walls at a pond

Walls
A tiered wall stepping up from a pond, plus pond walls with water at the base.
Challenge
In a tiered wall the upper wall loads the lower one, and the pond puts water at the toe. Checking each wall by itself is not enough.
Solution
The tiers were analyzed together for global stability, and the pond walls got their own schedule.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Oxenfree at Stonebriar

Frisco · Tiered, pond and building walls

Walls
A triple tiered wall at the pond, walls next to buildings, and walls sitting on rock.
Challenge
Three different conditions on one site: stacked walls at the water, building loads behind other walls, and rock showing up at the base.
Solution
Separate schedules for each condition, including a pond wall, a building surcharge wall and bearing on rock, so the crew could match the wall to what they found.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Northwood Club Pickleball Courts

Dallas · Two-tier vertical face walls

Walls
Two tiers of cut limestone with a vertical face, next to mature trees.
Challenge
A vertical face gives up the lean that helps a gravity wall, and the upper tier sits close enough to load the lower one.
Solution
Lower and upper tier schedules designed to work together, with a deepened footing on the upper tier and cross sections showing how they fit.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Lakeside at Silveron

Flower Mound · Stone walls with fire lane loads

Walls
Stone walls below a fire lane, some close to structures, with a fence on top.
Challenge
Fire trucks and nearby structures put extra load behind the wall, and the distance to each changed along the run.
Solution
Fire lane surcharge schedules and deepened footings that increase as a structure gets closer, with global stability checked on the tallest sections.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Silver Saddle Business Park Ph 1

Tarrant County · Walls on soil and rock

Walls
Walls along fire lanes and a pond, founded partly on soil and partly on rock.
Challenge
The foundation changed from soil to rock along the same wall, and the fire lane added load at the top.
Solution
Soil and rock versions of each wall schedule, with a rock cut at the base where needed and fire lane surcharge included.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Northpointe West

Fort Worth · Multi-phase stone walls

Walls
Battered and vertical face walls, walls behind houses and fire lanes, and walls on rock, across several phases.
Challenge
Every phase had a different mix of loads and foundations, and the site kept changing as phases were updated.
Solution
A set of schedules for each condition, carried through the revisions, with global stability checked where it mattered.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks

Wolf Creek Farms BTR

Melissa · Build-to-rent walls

Walls
Walls behind rental homes and along fire lanes, including deepened footing walls and a toe wall.
Challenge
Houses and fire lanes sit right behind the walls, so the wall type changes from lot to lot.
Solution
Building surcharge, fire lane surcharge and deepened footing schedules, and a toe wall added where the grading called for it.
Built by
C&W Stoneworks
