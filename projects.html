Featured walls

A few of the harder ones, and the problem each one had to solve. Built by C&W Stoneworks.

Valley View Industrial

Irving · Tall stone walls

Tall walls with buildings close behind them. At this height the question is whether the whole slope holds, not just the wall, so global stability was checked along the tallest runs.

Service: Retaining wall design · Global stability analysis

Acorn North Ph 2

Princeton · Tiered walls at a pond

A tiered wall stepping up from a pond. The upper wall loads the lower one and water sits at the toe, so the tiers were checked together for global stability.

Service: Global stability analysis

Oxenfree at Stonebriar

Frisco · Tiered, pond and building walls

A triple tiered wall at the pond, walls behind buildings and rock at the base, all on one site. Each condition got its own wall schedule so the crew could build to what they found.

Service: Retaining wall design

Lakeside at Silveron

Flower Mound · Walls below a fire lane

Stone walls below a fire lane with structures close behind. Footings get deeper as a structure gets closer, and global stability was checked on the tallest sections.

Service: Retaining wall design

Featured hardscape

Entry monuments, sign walls and screen walls, and what each one is built on.

Tavolo Park Ph 5

Fort Worth · Entry monument and screen wall

A stone entry monument with a standing seam metal roof that matches the earlier phases. The monument sits on a grade beam over drilled piers, with a 6 ft brick screen wall running out from it on both sides.

Service: Entry monuments, columns and signs · Screen wall design

Morningside Ph 1

Weatherford · Brick screen wall

A long brick screen wall with brick columns and stone caps. The panels span between drilled piers on a concrete grade beam, with openings left in the base course so water can drain through.

Service: Screen wall design

Westpoint Park

Fort Worth · Entry monument and screen wall

A stone entry monument carrying a weathering steel sign panel, and a 6 ft brick screen wall with brick columns. Both are founded on drilled piers.

Service: Entry monuments, columns and signs

Normandale Baptist Church

Fort Worth · Entry sign wall

A stucco sign wall on a stone base, with a stone column at one end. The wall and columns are block with stucco and stone veneer, founded on drilled piers.

Service: Entry monuments, columns and signs
