Retaining wall design

Retaining walls for subdivisions, commercial sites, apartments and residential lots, from short landscape walls to tall walls below roads, parking and buildings.

Project types
  • Stone and masonry gravity walls
  • Segmental block walls with geogrid
  • Cast-in-place concrete walls
  • Gabion walls
  • Shotcrete and gunite pond and channel walls
  • Tiered walls stepped up a slope
What you receive
  • Sealed wall plans with elevations, sections and details
  • Construction notes and material requirements
  • Calculations when the city or owner requires them
What we need from you
  • Grading plan with wall locations and top and bottom elevations
  • Geotechnical report, if available
  • The wall type the contractor plans to build
  • Anything near the top of the wall: fences, pools, drives, parking or buildings
Coordination
We work from the civil engineer's grading and drainage plan, match the landscape architect's or architect's layout and finish, and use the geotechnical engineer's soil information and recommendations when available.

Global stability analysis

A check of the slope, the wall and the soil together. Every project is screened; a detailed analysis is recommended for tall walls, tiered walls, walls on slopes and walls next to water.

Project types
  • Tall walls and tiered walls
  • Walls above or below steep slopes
  • Walls along ponds, creeks and channels
  • Walls designed by others that need the check
What you receive
  • A sealed analysis for the sections checked
  • Fix options when a section comes up short, reflected in the wall design
What we need from you
  • Geotechnical report with boring logs
  • Grading plan and wall sections
  • Loads near the top of the wall
Coordination
This work leans on the geotechnical engineer's soil data. We coordinate with them and the civil engineer when grades or drainage need to change.

Screen wall design

Brick, stone and CMU screen walls along subdivision perimeters, commercial property lines and thoroughfares, including the piers, columns and foundations.

Project types
  • Subdivision perimeter and thoroughfare walls
  • Commercial property line walls
  • Masonry walls with columns, on piers and grade beams
What you receive
  • Sealed plans with wall, column, pier and grade beam details
  • Reinforcing and construction notes
What we need from you
  • Layout plan showing wall runs, heights and finishes
  • Geotechnical report, if available
  • Gate, fence or light attachments on the wall
Coordination
We follow the landscape architect's layout and finish, check grades and utilities with the civil engineer, and base the foundations on the geotechnical report when one is available.

Construction observation

Observation of piers, footings and wall construction. This is a separate scope, not part of every design engagement, and is proposed on its own when a project needs it.

Project types
  • Drilled piers and footings before concrete
  • Wall construction, drainage and backfill
  • Remote documented photo review for projects outside DFW
What you receive
  • A written report after each observation visit, delivered to the party that engaged us
What we need from you
  • Sealed plans for the project
  • The construction schedule, with notice before pours and key stages
  • Site access and a contact on site
Coordination
We work with the contractor's superintendent on timing. Soil and concrete testing stays with the testing lab or geotechnical engineer.

Leaning and failing walls

Investigations of existing walls that are leaning, cracking or failing, with repair recommendations.

Project types
  • Leaning, bulging or cracked walls
  • Walls that moved after heavy rain
  • Walls built without plans
What you receive
  • A site review and a letter with findings and repair recommendations
What we need from you
  • Photos of the wall, the top and the toe
  • Approximate height and length
  • When the movement was first noticed
  • Any original plans or permits
Coordination
If the cause points to the soil, we bring in a geotechnical engineer for borings.

Completed project: Available on request. Existing wall reviews are kept private for the owner.
